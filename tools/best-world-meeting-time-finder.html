When this tool is useful

Use it when a team is spread across major cities and you want a quick first-pass answer before creating calendar invites. It is especially handy for recurring check-ins, interviews, and client calls across the US, Europe, and Asia.

Example use case

A founder in Tokyo wants to meet a customer in New York and a designer in London. Set Tokyo as the reference city, choose a candidate time such as 09:00 or 21:00, and check whether the other cities fall into a reasonable working or early-evening window before sending the invite.

FAQ

How is this different from a calendar app?

This is faster for a rough first pass. You pick one city, date, and time, then immediately see the matching local times across major hubs.

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses the browser time zone database through Intl.DateTimeFormat, so DST shifts are reflected automatically.

Who is this most useful for?

Remote teams, founders, recruiters, agencies, and anyone scheduling across the US, Europe, and Asia.

Should I still confirm the final meeting time?

Yes. This page is meant to narrow down candidate hours quickly. You should still confirm the final invite in your calendar system before sending it.
