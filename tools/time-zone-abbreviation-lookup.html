FAQ

Are time zone abbreviations always unique?

No. Some abbreviations can mean different things in different regions, so confirm critical times.

Does this replace a time zone database?

No. It is a quick lookup for common abbreviations.

Why do some entries mention daylight saving time?

Because abbreviations such as PST and PDT change depending on the season.
