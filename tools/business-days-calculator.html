Business Days Calculator

Calculate weekdays between two dates, excluding Saturday and Sunday.

How to use this tool

Calculate weekdays between two dates, excluding Saturday and Sunday.

When this tool is useful

Use this for project planning, payment windows, SLA estimates, hiring timelines, and workday-based deadlines.

Example use case

If a task starts Monday and is due next Friday, enter the dates to count the actual weekdays available for work.

FAQ

Does it handle public holidays?

No. This simple version excludes weekends only.

Can I include the end date?

Yes. Use the checkbox when the final day should count as a workday.

What counts as a business day?

Monday through Friday count as business days in this calculator.
