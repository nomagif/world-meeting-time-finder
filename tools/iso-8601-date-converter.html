FAQ

What is ISO 8601?

ISO 8601 is a common date and time format used by APIs, logs, and databases.

Does Z mean UTC?

Yes. A trailing Z means the timestamp is in UTC.

Does parsing happen locally?

Yes. The tool runs in your browser.
