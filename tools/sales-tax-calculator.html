FAQ

Does this know local tax rates?

No. Enter the rate you want to calculate.

Can it remove tax from a tax-included price?

Yes. Choose remove tax to estimate the pre-tax amount.

Can I use VAT?

Yes. The same math works for VAT-style percentages.
