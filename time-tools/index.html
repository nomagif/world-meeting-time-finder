Time tools

Free browser-based time tools for quick practical tasks.

  • Add Days to Date Calculator

    Add or subtract calendar days from a date and instantly see the resulting date and weekday.

  • Age Calculator

    Calculate exact age in years, months, and days from a birth date.

  • Business Days Calculator

    Count business days between two dates while excluding weekends and optionally including the end date.

  • Countdown Timer

    Create a simple countdown timer for minutes and seconds that runs directly in your browser.

  • Date Difference Calculator

    Calculate the number of days between two dates, including optional end-date inclusion.

  • Hours and Minutes Calculator

    Add or subtract hours and minutes for timesheets, schedules, and quick planning.

  • ISO 8601 Date Converter

    Parse an ISO 8601 date string or create the current ISO timestamp with local and UTC output.

  • Time Duration Calculator

    Calculate elapsed time between start and end times, including overnight durations.

  • Time Zone Abbreviation Lookup

    Look up common time zone abbreviations such as PST, EST, JST, UTC, CET, and AEST with quick notes and offsets.

  • Time Zone Meeting Planner by Cities

    Plan a meeting across selected cities and compare local times in one browser-only time zone planner.

  • Unix Timestamp Converter

    Convert Unix timestamps in seconds or milliseconds into readable local and UTC dates directly in your browser.

  • UTC to Local Time Converter

    Convert a UTC date and time to local times for major cities and your browser time zone in one simple page.

  • Week Number Calculator

    Find the ISO week number for a date and see the week start and end dates.

  • Work Hours Overlap Calculator

    Find overlapping work hours between selected cities using configurable local workday start and end times.
